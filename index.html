Denham Springs Police Department

Mobile App Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

The Denham Springs Police Department ("DSPD," "we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of users of the Denham Springs Police Department Mobile App ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected through the App, how that information may be used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you choose to use, the App may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Physical address
  • Photographs or other uploaded files
  • Information or written content submitted through App forms

This information may be provided through features such as requests for police services, tips, comments or complaints, employment-related submissions, and other department forms or services available through the App.

Certain authorized department users may also have accounts used to access administrative features of the App. Account-related information may include an email address, user or account identifier, role, and information necessary to authenticate and secure the account.

Push Notifications

The App may allow users to receive push notifications concerning department alerts, emergency information, city events, and other public information. The App may store notification preferences and technical information necessary to register a device for and deliver push notifications.

Users may disable notifications through the App where available or through their device's notification settings.

How We Use Information

Information collected through the App may be used to:

  • Provide requested App and department services
  • Receive, review, and respond to information submitted to DSPD
  • Process patrol requests, tips, comments, complaints, employment-related submissions, and other forms
  • Administer authorized user accounts
  • Provide and manage notifications
  • Maintain the security and operation of the App
  • Comply with applicable legal, administrative, records-retention, or law-enforcement requirements

DSPD does not use information collected through the App for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking, and DSPD does not sell personal information.

Information Sharing and Service Providers

Information submitted through the App may be accessible to authorized DSPD personnel as necessary to perform department functions.

DSPD may use third-party technology and service providers to operate App functionality, including infrastructure used for data storage, authentication, file storage, and push-notification delivery. Information may be processed by those providers when necessary to provide those services on DSPD's behalf.

Information may also be disclosed when required or permitted by applicable law, legal process, court order, public-records requirements, or legitimate law-enforcement or governmental purposes.

Data Retention

Information collected through the App may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected and as required by applicable laws, records-retention requirements, department policies, or legitimate law-enforcement and governmental needs.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the purpose for which it was submitted.

Data Security

DSPD uses reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information handled through the App. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

User Choices

Users are not required to use optional App submission features. When information is requested through a form, users should provide only information they are comfortable submitting and that is appropriate for the requested service.

Push notifications can be disabled through available App settings or through the device's operating-system settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended to provide public information and access to DSPD services. DSPD does not knowingly use the App to solicit personal information from children for advertising or marketing purposes. Parents or guardians who believe a child has submitted personal information through the App and have questions concerning that information may contact DSPD.

Public Records and Legal Requirements

Information submitted to a governmental or law-enforcement agency may be subject to applicable public-records, records-retention, evidentiary, investigative, or other legal requirements. Submission of information through the App does not guarantee that the information can be deleted upon request when DSPD is legally required or authorized to retain it.

Emergency Communications

This App is not monitored for emergency reporting.

Do not use the App to report an emergency or request immediate police assistance. Call 911 for emergencies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

DSPD may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, department practices, legal requirements, or technology. The effective date displayed at the top of this policy will indicate when it was most recently updated.

Contact Us

Questions concerning this Privacy Policy or information submitted through the App may be directed to:

Denham Springs Police Department
Denham Springs, Louisiana
